# Fixed ReclaimBay company roadmap

Product: **self-service automotive declined-work revenue scanner**.
Objective: **Profitable, recurring, progressively autonomous software revenue.**
The [company charter](COMPANY_CHARTER.md) governs all milestones. Acquisition and
outreach exist to market and acquire customers for this scanner.

## M1 — Strategic alignment

**Status: complete.** Product direction, business objective and the fixed M1–M5
roadmap are documented and linked from agent guidance. Unfinished replenishment
work is safely [parked](PARKED_REPLENISHMENT.md) outside active release inputs.

## M2 — Commercial readiness of the customer-facing scanner

**Status: current; local assessment in progress.** Begin with the existing scanner's
import, field mapping, calculations, duplicate treatment, results, exports,
privacy and self-service workflow. Identify existing payment and customer-access
capabilities and concrete obstacles to a customer using and paying for the scanner.
See the [scoped readiness assessment](M2_SCANNER_READINESS.md).

This assessment does not authorize scanner changes. Local tests or an assessment
report alone do not establish commercial readiness or complete M2.

## M3 — First Paying Customer

Acquire one real automotive repair business that successfully uses the ReclaimBay
scanner and makes a verified payment.

## M4 — Repeatable Revenue & Retention

Acquire at least three independent paying shops, demonstrate useful product
outcomes, and establish evidence for repeat purchases or recurring subscriptions.

## M5 — Scaled Autonomous Operations

Achieve positive owner-adjusted profit through dependable, progressively automated
customer acquisition, onboarding, analysis, billing, support, and monitoring, with
minimal routine owner involvement.

## Milestone discipline

M3–M5 are future milestones, not completed outcomes or invitations to expand scope
or build new features. Preserve the definitions and order. Evidence of successful
scanner use and verified payment is necessary for M3; acquisition counts and email
acceptance do not satisfy it. Do not infer subscriptions, retention or positive
owner-adjusted profit from activity alone.

No milestone definition grants permission to deploy, alter production, change
acquisition policy, activate replenishment, launch campaigns or send outreach.
Those actions retain their existing safeguards and authorization requirements.
